The Morning Dispatch

A personal newspaper. Each morning it gathers the newsletters and news feeds its owner already follows and builds one finite daily edition, with a clear place to finish.

The Morning Dispatch is a private, single-user project. It runs as a Google Apps Script web app and an Android app installed directly on its owner's own phones. It is not offered to the public, is not listed in any app store, and does not accept sign-ins from anyone other than its owner.

Signing in with Google. The app asks for Google permissions only so that its owner can read his own edition. Other Google accounts are refused.

See the privacy policy for what the app reads, where it keeps it, and how to remove its access.

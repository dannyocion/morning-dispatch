Privacy policy

The Morning Dispatch is a private app with one user: its owner. This page explains what it reads, where that information stays, and how to remove its access.

Effective: November 20, 2026.

Who can use it

Only the owner's Google account. The app's Google Apps Script service is set so that only its owner can run it, and requests from any other account are refused. The Android app is installed directly on the owner's own devices and is not distributed to anyone else.

What it reads, and why

  • Gmail (read-only): the newsletters the owner subscribes to, so they can appear in his daily edition. The app does not send, change or delete email.
  • Google Sheets: the app's own spreadsheet, where it stores headlines, short excerpts, links and the owner's reading settings.
  • YouTube data: public information about videos from channels the owner follows, such as titles and lengths.
  • Public news feeds and websites: headlines and summaries that publishers make publicly available.
  • Basic account information during sign-in: used only to confirm that the person signing in is the owner.

Where the information stays

  • In the owner's own Google account: his spreadsheet and the app's Apps Script settings.
  • On the owner's own Android devices: a copy of recent daily editions for offline reading, kept for up to 7 days.

The app has no other servers or databases, and it does not sell, rent or share this information with anyone.

AI processing

To label stories (for example, news report versus opinion), the app may send short excerpts of newsletter text, not full emails, to Google's Gemini API. This uses Google's unpaid tier, under which Google may use submitted content to improve its services, as described in Google's Gemini API terms. The excerpts are the owner's own subscribed content, and the feature can be switched off.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
  • Stored data lives in the owner's own Google spreadsheet and can be deleted there. Offline copies on a device are removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
